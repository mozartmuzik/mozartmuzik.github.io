Privacy Policy

My AIssistant · Last updated: 5 November 2026

My AIssistant (“the app”) is a macOS application made by MozartMuzik that exports audio from Pro Tools and delivers it to the user’s clients through the user’s own Google Drive and Gmail. This policy explains what data the app uses and how.

1. Where your data lives

The app runs entirely on your Mac. There is no My AIssistant server: we (the developer) do not receive, store or have access to your files, your Google data or your history. Deliveries, artists, templates and settings are stored only on your Mac. Your Google sign-in tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain on your Mac.

2. Google user data the app accesses

If you connect a Google account, the app requests these permissions:

  • Google Drive — files created or opened by the app (drive.file): to create a delivery folder, upload the audio files you export, share that folder with a link for your client, and remove it when the link expires or when you ask. The app cannot see or change any other file in your Drive.
  • Gmail — send email on your behalf (gmail.send): to send the delivery emails and reminders you request, to the recipients you choose, and (if you enable it) alerts to yourself. The app cannot read, search or delete your email.
  • Your email address (email): to show which account is connected and to use it as the sender of the delivery emails.

3. How the data is used

Google user data is used only to provide the features you use in the app: uploading your deliveries, sharing them and emailing your clients. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, and it is not used to train any artificial-intelligence or machine-learning model. It is not transferred to anyone, except the recipients you choose when you send a delivery.

My AIssistant’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Feedback and crash reports

Only if you choose to send them (Help › Send Feedback, or “Send Report” after a crash), the app emails the developer, from your Gmail, the message you write plus the app’s log and macOS crash report. These contain the names of sessions, tracks and files and technical details — never audio, passwords or Google tokens. Optional “stem reports” (only with your permission, changeable in Settings) contain the track names and routing of a session, to improve stem detection. You can ask us to delete anything you sent at the address below.

5. Keeping and deleting data

  • Files in your Google Drive delivery folder are deleted automatically after the period you set in Settings (7 days by default), or when you delete a delivery in the app.
  • Disconnecting your Google account in Settings deletes the app’s tokens from your Mac. You can also remove the app’s access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling the app and deleting its folder in ~/Library/Application Support removes all its local data.

6. Security

All communication with Google uses Google’s official APIs over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Tokens are kept in the macOS Keychain.

7. Children

The app is a professional tool and is not directed to children under 13.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
