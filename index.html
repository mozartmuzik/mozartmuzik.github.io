My AIssistant

An assistant for music producers and mix & mastering engineers who work in Pro Tools. Type what you need — “export the master, stems and multitracks of this song and email them to the artist” — and it does the rest.

What it does

  • Finds the Pro Tools session on your Mac or external drives and opens it.
  • Exports masters, stems (one per group) and multitracks, and checks them (true peak, silent files, stems that add up to the mix).
  • Uploads the files to a folder in your own Google Drive and creates a download link.
  • Sends the delivery email to your client from your own Gmail account, with an optional daily reminder until the link expires.
  • Keeps a history of every delivery on your Mac.

Google account access

When you connect your Google account, My AIssistant asks only for what it needs to deliver files on your behalf:

  • Google Drive (files created by the app): to upload your exports and share their folder with your client. The app cannot see any other file in your Drive.
  • Gmail (send only): to send the delivery emails you ask for. The app cannot read your inbox.
  • Your email address: to show which account is connected and to sign the emails.

Everything runs on your Mac. There is no My AIssistant server. Details in the Privacy Policy.
